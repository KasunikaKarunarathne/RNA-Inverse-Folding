Now the project
🚀 Project: QForge — Hybrid Quantum Computing Execution Platform
The basic idea:
Build a miniature version of the software stack that takes a real optimization problem, compiles it into a hardware-aware quantum circuit, schedules it across CPU/GPU/QPU backends, executes it, collects results, and benchmarks the entire workflow.
This is much more ambitious than a normal Qiskit project.
And that's exactly why it can become a portfolio centerpiece.

10. Stage 2 — Quantum Intermediate Representation
This is one of the most important parts.
Instead of immediately creating Qiskit circuits:
QuantumCircuit(...)
you create your own IR.
Something like:
Qubit 0 ── H ─────●──── RZ ───
                  │
Qubit 1 ── H ─────X──── RZ ───
Internally:
Instruction(
    opcode="CNOT",
    qubits=[0,1]
)
Your IR could support:
H
X
Y
Z
RX
RY
RZ
CNOT
CZ
MEASURE
Now you're doing real compiler engineering.

11. Stage 3 — Compiler passes
Implement actual passes.
Pass 1 — Gate cancellation
X ── X
becomes:
identity

Pass 2 — Rotation merging
RZ(a)
RZ(b)
becomes:
RZ(a+b)

Pass 3 — Remove redundant gates
H ─ H
→ nothing.

Pass 4 — Depth optimization
Take:
H q0
H q1
H q2
H q3
and schedule them simultaneously.

12. Stage 4 — Hardware-aware compiler

Your compiler needs to insert:

Now you implement:
